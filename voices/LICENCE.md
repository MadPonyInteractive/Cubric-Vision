# Cubric Vision Voice Licence 1.0

Applies to every voice clip in this directory (`*.opus`) and to the performance
clips in `performance/`. Identified in `manifest.json` as
`Cubric-Vision-Voice-Licence-1.0`.

## What these files are

Every clip was generated in-house by Mad Pony Interactive, or recorded by us.
None of it is sampled from a third-party voice library, and no clip is a
reproduction of any identifiable person's voice other than our own.

## The clips themselves: proprietary

The clips are proprietary to Mad Pony Interactive. They ship as part of Cubric
Vision so the app can use them. They are **not** offered as a voice library:
they may not be extracted, redistributed, resold, or published as a standalone
voice pack, dataset, or model training set.

## What you make with them: yours, commercial use included

Audio you generate in Cubric Vision using these voices is **yours**. You may use
it commercially, without royalty, attribution, or any further permission from
Mad Pony Interactive. We claim no rights in your output, and there is no
territory restriction.

This is the whole point of the distinction: we keep the voices, you keep the
work.

## Upstream

The models used to generate these voices impose no conditions on their outputs.
Checked 2026-08-26: Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign is Apache-2.0, which governs
the weights and code and makes no claim on generated audio. The ComfyUI wrappers
(GPL-3.0 and Apache-2.0) licence the code, not the audio it produces. Four clips
in `elderly_male` derive from a recording by Fabio Gonçalves, used with his
permission; two of those pass through Chatterbox, whose node is MIT.
