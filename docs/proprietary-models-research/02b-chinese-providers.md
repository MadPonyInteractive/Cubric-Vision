# Kling, MiniMax and ByteDance: the three models people actually asked for

Researched 2026-09-11 by live fetch. These three matter most because they are the models the
suggestion named, and they are also where the legal picture is worst. Read this before
promising any of them in a product.

**Short version: all three are cheap and all three are contractually unsafe to resell direct
without written permission.** Two of the three have terms that, read plainly, forbid exactly
what a credit-based app does. Buying them through an aggregator does not automatically fix
that, because every aggregator passes the upstream model licence through to you.

---

## 1. Kling (Kuaishou)

### Two products, do not conflate

- **kling.ai** is the international developer platform. This is the one a UK developer uses.
- **klingai.com** (and the mainland domain) is China-only, prices in CNY, different promotions.

UK signup on kling.ai is confirmed available with no geo-restriction, no company
registration, no VAT number and no Chinese presence. A credit card is enough.

### Pricing

Developer units are prepaid and completely separate from consumer memberships. Consumer
credits and API units cannot cross.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trial S | $9.80 | 100 | $0.098 | 30 days | 5 |
| Trial L | $98 | 1,000 | $0.098 | 30 days | 5 |
| Standard 1 | $700 | 5,000 | $0.14 | 180 days | 20 |
| Standard 2 | $2,100 | 15,000 | $0.14 | 180 days | 20 |
| Standard 3 | $5,670 | 45,000 | $0.126 | 180 days | 20 |
| Standard 4 | $7,560 | 60,000 | ~$0.126 | 180 days | 20 |

**Unused units do not roll over on expiry.** The volume discount is about 10 percent, from
$0.14 to $0.126, and it starts at a $5,670 commitment. That is a real cash-flow question for
a solo vendor: the cheap rate needs five figures up front and expires in 180 days.

Five-second clips: 720p no audio $0.42, 1080p no audio $0.56, 1080p with audio $0.63, 4K
$2.10. Images are $0.028 at 1K/2K and $0.056 at 4K.

**Failed API tasks do not consume credits.** Whether a moderation-rejected job is billed is
UNVERIFIED.

### The blocker, quoted

From the Terms of API Paid Service:

> "Each Paid Service and corresponding paid rights are limited to your own use through your
> registered User Account. Without written consent from the Company, users are prohibited
> from granting, borrowing, renting, transferring, selling or otherwise licensing the use of
> the User Account and Paid Services and all rights to others in any form."

And from the user ToS: section 3.1(d) forbids "market, rent or lease the Services for a fee
or charge" without written consent; section 4.6 forbids commercial use of Output without
authorisation, which paid plans grant.

Read plainly, "limited to your own use" blocks a product where our users submit prompts and
receive Kling video through our key. That is the definition of what we would be building.

There is real tension here: Kling's own documentation elsewhere refers to a "Client
Application" concept, which implies third-party apps are contemplated. The two readings do
not sit comfortably together, and the full API ToS page is JavaScript-rendered so only search
snippets could be retrieved. **A solicitor needs the complete document, and Kuaishou needs to
confirm the platform use case in writing, before anything ships.**

### Attribution

User ToS section 4.5 requires all generated content to display the "Kling AI" brand or logo,
and where labelling is impossible, to "prominently indicate that the Output is generated by
'Kling AI'." Paid consumer memberships remove the watermark. **Whether the API tier removes
the attribution obligation is UNVERIFIED** and it is not a small question: it would mean
every Kling video leaving Cubric Vision carries someone else's brand.

Output IP: section 4.4 says "You own all intellectual property rights ... in the Content."
Section 4.7.1 gives Kuaishou a non-exclusive royalty-free licence over submitted prompts and
images for "promotion, product/function upgrades, and research." No indemnity found.

## 2. MiniMax Hailuo

Operated by Nanonoble Pte. Ltd., a Singapore entity. International developer API at
platform.minimax.io. The consumer app at hailuoai.video is a separate product.

### The UK trap, and why it probably does not apply

The **MiniMax H3 model weight licence** for self-hosting explicitly excludes the **EU, UK,
South Korea and the US**. Section I.5 forbids use, reproduction, modification, distribution
or display of the H3 works or their outputs outside permitted territories.

The **hosted API** at platform.minimax.io is described as globally available with no
territorial carve-out. So the route for Cubric Vision is the hosted API, never the weights.
That distinction is worth writing down because it is easy to read "MiniMax excludes the UK"
and abandon a viable option.

### Pricing

Current model, MiniMax H3:

| Resolution | Duration | $/output second |
|---|---|---|
| 768P | 4 to 15s | $0.08 |
| 2K | 4 to 15s | $0.13 |
| 6th and later image reference | per reference | $0.04 |

Legacy Hailuo 2.x, billed per completed clip rather than per second:

| Model | 768p 6s | 768p 10s | 1080p 6s |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hailuo 2.3 Fast | $0.19 | $0.32 | $0.33 |
| Hailuo 2.3 | $0.28 | $0.56 | $0.49 |
| Hailuo 02 | $0.28 | $0.56 | $0.49 |

Effective per-second on the legacy models: Fast ~$0.032/s at 768p, Standard ~$0.047/s at
768p, ~$0.082/s at 1080p. **The legacy models are roughly half the price of current-gen H3.**

Enterprise monthly packages exist ($1,000 to $6,000/month for 3,760 to 26,780 units, 20 to 50
RPM), plus AWS Marketplace bundles from $10,000/month.

### Terms

The master terms are a framework that defers to product-specific terms at
platform.minimax.io/protocol/terms-of-service, which is JavaScript-rendered and could not be
read. That is the single most important unread document for this provider.

What the H3 weight licence says (governing self-hosting, possibly flowing through): section
V.2 requires every downstream user of a derived product to be bound to the same protections;
section V.5 requires "anti-abuse safeguards" with "a reporting channel and repeat-violator
suspensions"; revenue above $20M/year needs written authorisation. The hosted API path
reportedly has no revenue cap.

Notably, **no mandatory UI attribution is required for the hosted H3 API** (unlike the
self-hosted weights, which require "MiniMax H3" displayed prominently). That makes MiniMax
cleaner than Kling on branding.

Billing on failure: UNVERIFIED. Indemnity: UNVERIFIED.

## 3. ByteDance Seedance and Seedream

Two entirely separate platforms:

| Platform | Market | UK reachable | Signup |
|---|---|---|---|
| Volcano Engine (volcengine.com) | China domestic | **No** | Chinese business registration plus ID verification |
| BytePlus ModelArk (byteplus.com) | International | See conflict below | Credit card, self-serve |

Volcano Engine is not a viable path for a UK vendor. BytePlus is the only door.

### The UK availability conflict, unresolved

- BytePlus's **own availability page** lists the UK as a supported country. The US is **not**
  listed.
- At least one developer-facing source states the service is "not sold in the US, Canada, UK,
  Australia or New Zealand."

BytePlus's page carries its own caveat: "The availability of BytePlus Model Service in any
given country/region shall be determined at the point of purchase." So geo-gating may happen
at account creation even where a country is nominally listed.

**Action: try to create a BytePlus account from a UK address before planning anything around
Seedance.** This is a ten-minute test that settles a question no amount of reading will.

### The blocker, quoted

From the Specific Terms for the BytePlus Video Generation Model Services:

> "BytePlus grants customers a non-exclusive, non-transferable, **non-sublicensable**,
> revocable, and limited license to use the BytePlus Video Generation Model Services."

"Non-sublicensable" in API licensing usually means you cannot grant a third party the right
to call the API directly. It does not normally stop you building a product whose users
consume outputs, which is how most SaaS works. But the exact scope depends on the rest of the
document, and that document is JavaScript-rendered and could not be read in full.

A separate finding from the legal research: BytePlus imposes **20 percent liquidated damages
for "unauthorized resale of APIs."** That raises the cost of guessing wrong. There is also an
unverified, search-only clause banning certain "Restricted Models" across the entire EU
market, which may or may not touch Seedance.

Attribution, output ownership, moderation duties and failure billing for BytePlus are all
UNVERIFIED. The Platform Customer Code of Conduct page did not return readable content.

## 4. Summary for a UK solo vendor

| Factor | Kling | MiniMax Hailuo | BytePlus Seedance/Seedream |
|---|---|---|---|
| UK self-serve signup | Yes, confirmed | Yes for the API; **no** for H3 weights | **Uncertain, test at purchase** |
| Card only, no company | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| End users generating through our app | **Blocked without written consent** | UNVERIFIED, terms unreadable | **"Non-sublicensable", needs a solicitor** |
| Attribution required | Yes on consumer; API scope UNVERIFIED | **No** for hosted H3 API | UNVERIFIED |
| Output ownership | User owns; Kuaishou gets a promotion licence | User owns | UNVERIFIED |
| Failed jobs billed | **No** | UNVERIFIED | UNVERIFIED |
| Cheapest entry | $9.80 trial pack | $0.08/s pay-as-you-go | $0 free tier, then $0.035/image |
| Cheapest 5s video | $0.42 at 720p | ~$0.16 at 768p on Hailuo 2.3 Fast | ~$0.30 at 720p on Seedance 2.0 Fast |

**On price alone MiniMax wins and has the cleanest attribution terms.** Kling is the one with
the explicit written-consent requirement. ByteDance is the one where we cannot yet confirm a
UK account can even be opened.

## 5. What to do next, in order

1. **Open a BytePlus account from the UK.** Ten minutes, settles the availability conflict.
2. **Email Kuaishou developer support on kling.ai** asking for written consent for the
   specific case: a third-party desktop app where end users submit prompts and receive
   generated video through our API key. Do not build on Kling until that reply exists.
3. **Sign in to platform.minimax.io and export the full developer ToS** (it only renders
   signed in). Have a solicitor read it, and clarify whether H3 licence sections V.2 and V.5
   reach hosted-API users or only self-hosters.
4. **Get a solicitor onto the BytePlus Specific Terms** to scope "non-sublicensable", given
   the 20 percent liquidated-damages clause.
5. Consider whether an aggregator changes any of this. It does not remove the upstream
   licence, but it does mean the aggregator, not us, holds the direct relationship, and some
   aggregators (Higgsfield with Seedance, for example) clearly have negotiated channel deals.

## Open questions

- Full text of Kling's API Paid Service Protocol, and whether written consent is routinely
  granted, at what cost, and how long it takes.
- Whether Kling's API tier removes the section 4.5 attribution obligation.
- Full text of platform.minimax.io/protocol/terms-of-service.
- Whether a UK BytePlus account can actually be created and billed.
- Full text of the BytePlus Specific Terms for Video Generation Model Services.
- Billing on moderation rejection for all three.
- Whether any of the three offers indemnity. None found so far.
- What the Chinese providers' data-transfer position means under UK GDPR, which
  `03-legal-and-contracts.md` flags as the real landmine: there is no adequacy decision for
  China, and regulators have already fined on this exact transfer pattern.
